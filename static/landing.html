Frequently asked questions

Is DocChat free?

Yes. DocChat is free to use. You just sign in with your Google account.

What files can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), plain text, Markdown, CSV and images (PNG, JPG, WebP), up to 10 MB per chat.

Can other people see my documents?

No. Your chats and files are tied to your account and only you can see them. You can delete any chat at any time. See the privacy policy.

How does DocChat answer questions?

DocChat uses Google's Gemini AI to read your document and answer based on its content, with references to where the answer came from.

Should I upload confidential documents?

Please don't. DocChat is a free service and is not designed for confidential or sensitive material.
