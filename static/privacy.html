Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

DocChat lets you upload documents and ask questions about them. This page explains what data DocChat uses and why. DocChat is a free personal project, not a commercial service.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: when you sign in with Google, DocChat receives your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep your chats separate from everyone else's.
  • Your documents and chats: files you upload, the questions you ask and the answers you get are stored on the DocChat server so you can come back to them.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, the server sees your IP address, which may be used to prevent abuse.

How your documents are processed

To answer your questions, your documents and questions are sent to Google's Gemini API, which processes them under Google's Gemini API terms. Because DocChat uses Gemini's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products. Please don't upload confidential or sensitive documents.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't access your Google Drive, Gmail or any other Google data. Sign-in only shares your name, email and picture.
  • We don't show your chats or documents to other users.

How long data is kept

DocChat runs on a free hosting plan, so stored chats and documents are regularly wiped when the server restarts. You can also delete any chat (and its files) yourself at any time with the trash button. To remove your account record, contact us below.

Cookies

DocChat uses a cookie only to keep you signed in and to remember your chats. Your theme choice is saved in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
